Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Meridian Properties ("we", "us", "our") operates the Meridian Properties property management platform. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

1. Who we are

Meridian Properties, Westlands, Nairobi, Kenya. For privacy questions, contact info@meridianproperties.co.ke.

2. Data we collect

Account data (from property managers and agency staff)

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Your role within your agency
  • Organisation name and contact details
  • Password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text)

Tenant data (entered by property managers)

  • Tenant name, email, phone number
  • Unit and property assignment
  • Rent, deposit, and payment history
  • Maintenance requests they submit
  • Documents uploaded on their behalf

Technical data

  • IP address and browser information (for security and debugging)
  • Timestamps of logins and actions (for audit and fraud prevention)

3. Why we collect it

We process personal data to:

  • Provide the property management service you have signed up for
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts from unauthorised access
  • Generate receipts, statements, and reports you request
  • Send service-related notifications (password resets, receipts)
  • Comply with legal and tax obligations

4. Who can see your data

Who Can see
You Your own account data
Your agency's managers and directors All data within your agency
Your assigned property manager Your tenant record, unit, payments, maintenance requests
Other tenants in the same agency Nothing about you (strict per-tenant isolation)
Meridian Properties staff Only what is needed for support, when you ask us for help

5. Third parties

We use the following third-party services to operate the platform:

  • MongoDB Atlas - database hosting
  • Vercel - application hosting
  • Resend - transactional email (when enabled)
  • Safaricom M-Pesa - payment processing (when enabled)

Each provider processes data under their own privacy terms. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

6. How long we keep it

  • Active account data: as long as your account exists
  • Payment and receipt records: 7 years (Kenyan tax record requirement)
  • Deleted accounts: purged within 30 days, except tax-related records
  • Server logs: 90 days

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances
  • Complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner

To exercise any of these rights, email info@meridianproperties.co.ke. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, session tokens with expiry, and access controls that limit who can see what. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your information.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date and notify account holders of significant changes by email.
